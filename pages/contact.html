Before You Visit

What to keep ready for a useful first call

A useful first call is specific. Whether you are buying a plot, comparing construction quotes or planning a build on land you already own, a few details help us give a real answer instead of a brochure. Nothing here is mandatory, but enquiries that include these points are usually resolved in one conversation.

For a property enquiry

  • Preferred city and locality, plus any areas you want to avoid.
  • Budget band and whether a home loan is required.
  • Plot size or carpet area you are targeting.
  • Purchase timeline and whether the property is for self-use or investment.

For a construction quote

  • Plot address, dimensions and road access for material delivery.
  • Whether the map is sanctioned, applied for or not yet started.
  • Built-up area per floor and the number of floors planned.
  • Specification level: Standard, Premium or Luxury.
  • Whether boundary wall, borewell, septic tank and external works are included.

Response times

Calls and emails are answered on the same working day. Site visits in Mirzapur, Varanasi and Prayagraj are usually arranged within 48 hours. A written construction estimate follows within three to five working days of the site visit, once drawings and specifications are confirmed. Documentation queries are routed to the concerned project manager rather than handled by a call centre.

Where we meet clients

Our head office is in Mirzapur. Site offices and project visits operate in Varanasi, Prayagraj and Lucknow, and we meet clients in Kanpur, Agra, Ayodhya, Noida, Ghaziabad, Gorakhpur, Bareilly and Jhansi by appointment. Office hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Sunday visits are possible for outstation buyers if arranged in advance.
