Tender to Handover

How public works are executed

Government and institutional work rewards preparation more than speed. A school building, primary health centre or departmental office has to meet the same structural codes as a private home, but with tighter documentation, measurement discipline and audit trails. Our approach is to settle the paperwork before the first excavator reaches the site.

We work as a registered civil contractor on PWD, state department and local body tenders, and as an execution partner for institutions that fund their own buildings. Scope ranges from new construction to renovation, boundary walls, drainage, approach roads and site development. Projects are run by a dedicated project manager with separate quantity, safety and quality responsibilities.

The tender-to-handover sequence

1. NIT and BoQ review. We read the tender documents, visit the site and flag ambiguities before pricing.

2. Rate analysis and bid. Labour, material, plant, overheads and risk are priced separately so the bid is defensible.

3. Award and agreement. Insurance, performance security, mobilisation and site handover are completed in order.

4. Execution. Measurement books, material tests, labour registers and progress photographs are maintained from day one.

5. Inspection and billing. Stage inspections and running bills follow the agreement, with variations recorded before work is taken up.

6. Completion. As-built drawings, completion certificate and defect-liability support are handed to the department.

We can review drawings and scope, prepare a phased budget and execute against a committee-approved schedule.
