Delivery Approach

From drawing to handover

A project page shows finished buildings. What determines whether a home is worth living in is the sequence behind it: how the site was surveyed, how the structure was cured, how the finishing trades were sequenced, and how defects were closed before the keys were handed over.

Our residential work covers plotted townships, apartment buildings, villas and independent homes. Commercial work includes showrooms, office floors and mixed-use buildings. Each project is run by a project manager with a site engineer and supervisor, and reviewed weekly against drawings, cost and timeline. Buyers receive stage photographs and a written progress note rather than a verbal update at the end of the month.

Milestones we track

1. Feasibility and title review. Location, access, land use and ownership verified before design begins.

2. Design and approvals. Architectural drawings, structural design, soil test and map sanction.

3. Foundation and frame. Excavation, footing, plinth, columns and slabs with stage inspections.

4. Masonry, MEP and plaster. Brickwork, plumbing, electrical, waterproofing and internal finishes.

5. Finishing and handover. Flooring, joinery, painting, testing, snag closure and documentation.

What handover includes

Every completed home is handed over with as-built drawings, structural and material test reports, warranty documents, maintenance notes and a named service contact. A snag list is closed before possession, not after. The structural warranty stated in the contract is documented separately so the owner knows what is covered and for how long.
