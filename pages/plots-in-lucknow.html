LUCKNOW PLOT FAQS

Questions About Buying or Renting Plots in Lucknow

1. What is the process of registry and mutation for plots in Lucknow?

Once the sale deed is executed at the sub-registrar office (Sadar, Mohanlalganj, or Bakshi Ka Talab depending on jurisdiction), Prakhar India assists with municipal or tehsil Dakhil Kharij (mutation) to transfer official Khatauni records in the buyer's name within 45 days.

2. Can we start turnkey house construction right after plot purchase?

Yes! Prakhar India provides an integrated package: buy the plot and immediately commission our in-house civil engineering team to build your dream 2BHK/3BHK villa at ₹1,550 to ₹2,450/sq.ft with structural warranty.

3. What terms apply for commercial plot rentals in Lucknow?

Commercial plot leases usually feature an initial 3 to 15-year tenure with standard 5% to 7% annual escalation, registered rent agreement, and permissions for pre-engineered steel structures, fencing, and high-load power connectivity.
