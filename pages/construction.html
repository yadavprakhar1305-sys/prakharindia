Scope, Rates & Process

What a turnkey contract actually covers

Turnkey means one contract covers design, approvals, structure, finishes and handover. The rate depends on specification, plot access and structural span, so a headline number is only useful once the drawing is known. The table below shows our current residential bands per square foot of built-up area. Final quotes follow a site inspection and drawing review.

The six-stage build process

1. Site visit and brief. We check access, levels, soil history, neighbouring structures and the family's room requirement.

2. Survey, soil test and concept. A topographic survey and bearing capacity test inform the foundation and the first floor plan.

3. Structural design and fixed-price contract. Drawings, specifications, payment milestones and the rate are frozen in writing.

4. Approvals and mobilisation. Map sanction, labour induction, material scheduling and site setup happen before excavation.

5. Execution and milestone billing. Stage inspections at footing, plinth, each slab, masonry, MEP and plaster; bills follow completed stages, not calendar months.

6. Finishing and handover. Snag list, testing, cleaning, as-built drawings, warranty documents and maintenance notes are handed over with the keys.

What we check before every pour

  • Reinforcement diameter, spacing and cover against the structural drawing.
  • Shuttering alignment, level and props, including access for vibration.
  • Electrical conduits, plumbing sleeves and embedded items in position.
  • Concrete mix design, slump and cube sampling for the pour.
  • Curing schedule and protection for the following seven days.

Frequently asked questions

What does turnkey construction cost in Mirzapur?

Our current bands are ₹1,450 per sq.ft for Standard, ₹1,850 for Premium and ₹2,400 and above for Luxury. The final figure depends on soil, design, material selection and site access.

How long does a 1,000 sq.ft house take?

A G+1 home of that size typically takes eight to ten months from mobilisation, subject to approvals, weather and payment milestones. A single-floor home is usually faster.

Do you handle map approval?

Yes. We prepare the drawings and liaise with the local authority. Statutory fees and any compounding charges are billed at actuals with receipts.

Can I supply my own material?

Yes, on a labour-only or partial-material scope. The contract is revised to reflect the reduced scope, and material quality responsibility shifts to the owner for the supplied items.
