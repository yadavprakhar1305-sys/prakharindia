One Team, Three Practices

Real estate, construction and advisory

Most buyers in Uttar Pradesh deal with a broker for the plot, an architect for the drawings, a contractor for the structure and a separate vendor for finishing. Every handover becomes a negotiation between people who have never met. Prakhar India was set up in Mirzapur in 2014 to close those gaps. We develop and sell land, build on it with our own engineers and site teams, and stay involved until the family moves in.

The real estate practice handles plotted developments, residential townships, villas and commercial units across Mirzapur, Varanasi, Prayagraj and Lucknow. The construction practice takes turnkey residential and commercial contracts from excavation to final paint, including architectural drawings, structural design, map approvals and MEP work. The advisory practice helps families compare localities, prepare loan documentation and plan a build in phases instead of committing everything at once.

That combination matters most when a customer buys a plot from us and then builds on it. The soil test, setback rules and structural drawings are already familiar ground, so there is no gap between the seller's promise and the builder's quote. The same project manager who answered questions at the booking stage is accountable at slab casting and handover.

How we measure ourselves

We count completed handovers rather than enquiries, and repeat referrals rather than advertising reach. More than 328 homeowners and commercial clients have taken possession of a Prakhar India property or building. Our internal review looks at three things on every project: whether the structure passed its stage inspections, whether the handover happened on the agreed date, and whether the owner would recommend us to a relative.
