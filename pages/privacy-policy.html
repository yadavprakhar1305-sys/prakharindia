Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Prakhar India Real Estate & Construction ("Prakhar India", "we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our office, why we collect it, how it is used and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Contact details such as name, mobile number, email address and city.
  • Property requirements, budget range, preferred locality and construction specifications.
  • Site visit addresses and appointment preferences.
  • Documents you choose to share for booking, loan coordination or registration, such as identity proof, address proof and income documents.
  • Correspondence with our team, including emails, call notes and grievance records.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type and pages visited, where analytics is enabled.

How we collect it

We collect information when you submit a website enquiry form, call or email us, visit our office or a project site, respond to an advertisement, or share documents during a booking or construction contract. We do not buy personal data from data brokers.

Why we use it

  • Respond to enquiries and arrange site visits.
  • Prepare property options, construction estimates and written quotations.
  • Process bookings, agreements, payments and registration formalities.
  • Coordinate with lenders, advocates and statutory authorities where you ask us to.
  • Provide warranty, maintenance and defect-liability support after handover.
  • Meet accounting, tax and legal record-keeping obligations.
  • Send service updates about an active project. Marketing messages are sent only where permitted, and you can opt out at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. It is shared only where needed to deliver the service: with advocates, chartered accountants, banks and housing finance companies, architects and structural consultants, contractors and material suppliers, and statutory authorities such as the registration office or UP-RERA. Service providers who process data on our behalf are required to keep it confidential and use it only for the assigned task.

Cookies and third-party content

This website uses minimal cookies needed for basic functionality. The contact page embeds a Google Maps frame, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When those elements load, Google may receive your IP address and browser information under its own privacy policy. We do not control third-party cookies; you can block them in your browser settings, though the map may then not display.

Retention and security

Enquiry data is retained while the enquiry is active and for up to twenty-four months afterwards for follow-up and record purposes. Booking, payment and contract records are retained for the period required by tax and real estate law. Access is limited to staff who need the information, and records are stored on access-controlled systems with backups. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please share only the documents that are necessary.

Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask for correction of inaccurate details, withdraw consent for marketing, or request deletion where no legal obligation requires retention. Write to prakharindiaofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request". We aim to respond within thirty days. If you are not satisfied, you may raise the matter with the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

Our services are directed at adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under eighteen. If a minor's details have been submitted, contact us and we will delete them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our processes or the law change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new date. Material changes affecting an active booking will also be communicated to the concerned customer.
