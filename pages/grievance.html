Grievance Redressal

Prakhar India Real Estate & Construction is committed to resolving complaints about property bookings, construction quality, site conduct, billing and documentation through a defined process. This page explains what you can raise, how to file it and what response to expect.

What you can raise

  • Booking, cancellation, refund or transfer of a plot, home or commercial unit.
  • Construction delay, workmanship, material quality or deviation from approved drawings.
  • Billing, payment schedule or invoice discrepancies.
  • Conduct of site staff, contractors or sales representatives.
  • Handover documentation, warranty claims or maintenance during the defect-liability period.
  • Privacy or data correction requests relating to your personal information.

How to file a complaint

Email prakharindiaofficial@gmail.com with the subject line "Grievance - [project or booking reference]". You may also call +91 90444 99111 or write to the Grievance Officer, Prakhar India Real Estate & Construction, Plot No. 14, Industrial Area, Mirzapur City, Uttar Pradesh 231001.

Please include your name, contact number, booking or project reference, the dates involved, a short description of what happened, copies of relevant documents and the remedy you are seeking. If a site inspection is needed, mention the best days for access.

What happens next

Acknowledgement. We acknowledge a written complaint within two working days and issue a reference number.

Preliminary response. The concerned project manager reviews the record and responds within seven working days.

Resolution. We aim to close most complaints within thirty days. Cases needing third-party approvals, structural inspection or legal opinion may take up to forty-five days, with a written status update every fortnight.

If you are not satisfied

For a project registered under UP-RERA, you may approach the UP-RERA authority after the internal process. For consumer disputes, the appropriate consumer commission is available under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. We will provide the project documents you are entitled to so the complaint can be assessed on facts.

Records and confidentiality

Complaint records are retained for three years after closure and are accessible only to the staff handling the matter. We do not publish complainant identities. Retaliation against a complainant or a site employee who raises a concern is treated as a disciplinary matter.
