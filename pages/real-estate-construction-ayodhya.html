EXPERT ADVISORY

Frequently Asked Questions: Ayodhya Property & Construction

1. Can outsiders or NRI investors buy land and property in Ayodhya?

Yes, Indian citizens and NRIs can legally purchase freehold residential and commercial property in Ayodhya under Uttar Pradesh property registration laws. Prakhar India provides complete assistance with title verification, 143 conversion documents, and registry execution at the Ayodhya Sadar sub-registrar office.

2. What permissions are required to build a hotel or guest house in Ayodhya?

Constructing a commercial hotel or guest house requires ADA (Ayodhya Development Authority) building map sanction, Section 143 non-agricultural conversion order, Fire Safety NOC, UPPCB environmental clearance, and electricity department load sanctions. Prakhar India handles all of these statutory approvals under our turnkey civil contracting contract.

3. How does turnkey construction work for remote/NRI investors in Ayodhya?

Our turnkey construction process is 100% transparent: we provide live weekly CCTV feeds, photographic progress logs, milestone-linked escrow payment terms, and deliver the project with complete occupancy certificate (OC) and structural warranty.
