Build Your Career With Prakhar India

We are hiring Civil Engineers, Site Supervisors, Property Consultants, and Architects.

Working at Prakhar

Site experience you can build on

Construction careers are often described in terms of offices and software. Ours is different. Prakhar India runs live residential, commercial and institutional sites across Uttar Pradesh, and most decisions are made where the work happens: at the footing, on the slab, during the finishing stage. If you want responsibility early and are willing to learn from measured work, this is a useful place to grow.

A civil engineer here owns a package rather than a spreadsheet. You will read structural drawings, check reinforcement before a pour, maintain cube test records and explain progress to a family that has saved for years to build one home. Site supervisors run labour gangs, material receipts and safety checks. Property advisors handle site visits, documentation and loan coordination with banks.

Live projects

Residential, commercial and government sites across twelve UP cities.

In-house design

Architects, structural engineers and execution teams review drawings together.

Measured quality

IS 456 concrete checks, steel verification and stage-wise inspection records.

Clear growth path

Site engineer to project manager; property advisor to sales lead.

Roles we hire for

We regularly recruit civil engineers and site managers, architects and 3D designers, site supervisors, property advisors and sales managers, quantity surveyors, and accounts or documentation executives. Final-year students from civil engineering and architecture colleges in Mirzapur, Varanasi and Prayagraj are considered for internships, with a preference for candidates who can commit to a full site cycle rather than a short desk assignment.

How the hiring process works

1. Application. Use the form below or email your CV to prakharindiaofficial@gmail.com with the role in the subject line.

2. Screening call. A short conversation within three working days covering experience, current location and notice period.

3. Technical or site discussion. A drawing review, quantity check or site walk-through with the project manager, depending on the role.

4. Offer. Written offer with role, reporting site, salary band and joining date. We do not charge candidates at any stage.

Include your CV, current city, notice period, expected salary and one project you handled end to end. A short note on what went wrong on that project is more useful to us than a list of software skills.
