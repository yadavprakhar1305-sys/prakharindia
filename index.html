Compliance & Client Assurance

Governance, Contracts & Buyer Assurance

How are construction rates fixed — do material price fluctuations increase our budget later?

Every turnkey project is governed by a stamped fixed-rate Bill of Quantities (BOQ) mentioning steel, cement, tile, plumbing, and electrical brands. Once signed, Prakhar India absorbs steel/cement price swings within the locked specification scope.

Are Mirzapur and Lucknow residential plots bank loan and immediate registry ready?

Yes. Enclaves in Vindhyachal Road, Natwa Cantt, and Shastri Bridge carry verified Section 143 / freehold chains eligible for Dakhil Kharij (mutation) and SBI/HDFC financing upon booking.

Do I pay full construction fees upfront or per milestone stage?

Never upfront. Billing strictly follows engineering progress milestones: 10% mobilization, foundation clearance, plinth completion, slab casting stages, masonry/MEP, and finishing handover.

Does Prakhar India undertake Government & institutional civil infrastructure tenders?

Yes — our EPC infrastructure team executes institutional buildings, boundary works, and commercial complexes under government and PSU compliance norms.
