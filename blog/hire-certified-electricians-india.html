How to Hire Certified Electricians & ITI Technicians for Your Factory

Technical Hiring Updated: September 2026 By Technical Cell, Prakhar India

Electrical safety is non-negotiable in manufacturing facilities, power distribution setups, commercial complexes, and high-voltage construction installations. Employing uncertified or semi-trained wiremen poses severe risks of short circuits, industrial fires, transformer damage, and legal penalties under the Central Electricity Authority (Measures Relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations.

At Prakhar India, we deploy vetted electricians, ITI wiremen, and electrical supervisors across UP and Delhi-NCR. In this hiring guide, we outline the exact verification steps every operations manager must follow.

1. Certifications to Verify Before Hiring

  • ITI Certificate in Electrical Trade: 2-year National Council for Vocational Training (NCVT) or State Council (SCVT) certification ensuring grounded knowledge in single-phase and three-phase circuits.
  • State Electrical Wireman Permit / License: Issued by the Electrical Licensing Board (UP Vidyut Suraksha Nideshalaya), certifying practical competency.
  • Electrical Supervisor Certificate of Competency (Class A or B): Mandatory for personnel supervising high-tension (HT) sub-stations, control panels, and generator switchgear.

2. Skill Matrix for Different Electrical Roles

  1. Industrial Panel Board Electrician: Capable of reading wiring schematics, motor control center (MCC) troubleshooting, PLC interface wiring, and cable terminations.
  2. Construction & Building Wireman: Specialized in conduit laying, modular switch fitting, distribution board (DB) dress-up, and earthing pit installations. Learn more on our Civil & Commercial Construction page.
  3. Sub-station & Transformer Attendant: Trained in oil testing, breaker testing (VCB/ACB), HT cable jointing, and load balancing.
