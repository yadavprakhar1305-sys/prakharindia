Homeowners starting residential builds across Sarnath, Shivpur, Ramnagar, Lanka, and BHU colonies frequently ask why VDA (Varanasi Development Authority) map approval drags on for months when neighboring builders seem to start immediately. When our engineering desk executes turnkey house construction in Varanasi, building plan sanction runs cleanly in parallel with geotechnical soil testing through the Uttar Pradesh Online Building Plan Approval System (OBPAS / UPVAS).

Exact Documents We Submit in a Residential VDA Application

Every clean residential G+1 or duplex submission combines ownership verification, revenue-code land-use status, and AutoCAD / PreDCR-layered architectural and structural files certified by VDA-empanelled professionals. Missing a single revenue extract or setback dimension forces the automated scrutinizer to bounce the application back before an officer even reviews it.

Two Common VDA Rejection Reasons We Prevent Upfront

1. Abutting Road Width & Front Setback Mismatches Under Varanasi Master Plan 2031: Many colony plots marketed as having a "25-foot road" measure 18 or 20 feet in official Sajra maps or fall inside proposed Master Plan road widening zones. Because permissible Floor Area Ratio (FAR), height, and mandatory front setback depend strictly on verified revenue-road width rather than site claims, drawing 100% ground coverage causes instant OBPAS rejection. Measuring physical corners against VDA zoning sheets before finalizing the floor plan avoids redrawing twice.

2. Overlooking Heritage & Flood-Plain Buffer Restrictions: Plots falling within 100 meters (prohibited) or 100–300 meters (regulated) of ASI-protected monuments around Sarnath, Lal Khan Tomb, or Ramnagar, as well as sites close to High Flood Level (HFL) marks along the Ganga and Varuna rivers, require NMA / Irrigation clearances and strict height ceilings. Trying to file standard G+2 multi-unit plans in a heritage-regulated pocket stalls approval indefinitely unless clearances are sequenced before fee submission.

Timeline, Treasury Fee Heads & Pre-Construction Site Readiness

In real field practice across Shivpur, Tarna, Sarnath, and Ramnagar, statutory VDA scrutiny typically settles in 30 to 45 working days when revenue conversion (Section 80 UP Revenue Code) and PreDCR digital drawings match physical site measurements cleanly. Delays occur when colony layout plans lack approved 9-meter or 12-meter feeder roads, or when adjacent plot boundary coordinates conflict with current Tehsil Sajra records.

Homeowners should also distinguish three statutory outflows during online demand note generation: (1) VDA scrutiny and stacking fees calculated per square meter of proposed built-up area, (2) external development or betterment charges tied to Master Plan infrastructure zones, and (3) mandatory rainwater harvesting and solar thermal undertaking deposits refunded upon completion certificate (CC) compliance. Paying these treasury challans directly via the official UPVAS homeowner login keeps receipts transparent and audit-ready for home loan tranches.
