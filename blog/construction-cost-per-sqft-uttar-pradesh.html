Construction Cost Per Sq Ft in Uttar Pradesh 2026: Complete Rate Guide

Construction Economics Updated: September 2026 By Prakhar India Estimators

Planning residential or commercial construction in Uttar Pradesh requires realistic budget forecasting. Over the past two years, material fluctuations, revised UP minimum wage schedules, and GST regulations have changed baseline construction figures.

As a registered civil contractor executing turnkey building projects and workforce deployment across UP, Prakhar India presents this verified 2026 construction rate handbook for builders, investors, and homeowners.

1. Overview of Building Rates Per Sq Ft in UP (2026)

2. Material vs Labour Cost Breakdown

On any standard civil site in Uttar Pradesh, total expenses divide into:

  • Building Materials (60% – 65%): Cement, Fe-550D TMT steel bars, red clay bricks, river sand / grit, stone ballast, plumbing, and electrical conduits.
  • Workforce & Labour Wages (25% – 30%): Mistri (masons), shuttering carpenters, bar benders, daily wage labourers, and site supervisors. You can review our manpower supply services for bulk deployment.
  • Contractor Margin & Architecture Fees (8% – 12%): Blueprint designs, municipal approvals, engineering oversight, and profit margins.

4. City-wise Cost Variations in Uttar Pradesh

Location impacts material transit costs and daily wage scales:

  • Noida & Ghaziabad (NCR): Highest labour costs, but faster material availability. Average: ₹1,650 – ₹2,200/sqft. Explore our Noida labour contractor options.
  • Lucknow & Kanpur: Balanced hub with competitive cement and steel logistics. Average: ₹1,450 – ₹1,850/sqft. See Lucknow workforce.
  • Mirzapur & Sonbhadra: Proximity to stone aggregates and mining reduces ballast costs, though specialized finishing artisans may command a premium. Average: ₹1,400 – ₹1,800/sqft. See Mirzapur construction hub.
  • Varanasi & Prayagraj: High inner-city density requires specialized transport. Average: ₹1,500 – ₹1,900/sqft. See Varanasi construction services.
