2. Structural Core vs Finishing Cost Breakdown

On any standard civil site in Uttar Pradesh, total expenses divide into:

  • Structural Core (40% – 45%): Cement, Fe-550D TMT steel bars, red clay bricks, river sand, RCC foundation, and columns.
  • Masonry & Plastering (18% – 22%): Brick walls, sand plaster, external weatherproofing, and foundation backfilling.
  • Finishing & Interiors (28% – 32%): Vitrified/marble tiles, flush doors, UPVC windows, interior paint, and sanitary fittings.
  • Electrical & Plumbing (10% – 12%): Concealed wiring, distribution boards, switches, CPVC piping, and fixture installations.

3. City-wise Variations in Uttar Pradesh

Location impacts material transit costs and logistical availability:

  • Lucknow & NCR: Average: ₹1,650 – ₹2,200/sqft. High material availability and modern high-rise finishing standards.
  • Varanasi & Prayagraj: Average: ₹1,500 – ₹1,900/sqft. Excellent connectivity for cement, steel, and stone supplies.
  • Mirzapur & Sonbhadra: Proximity to stone aggregates reduces foundation ballast costs. Average: ₹1,450 – ₹1,800/sqft. See our Mirzapur Hub.
