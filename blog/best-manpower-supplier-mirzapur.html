1. Check EPF & ESI Compliance ✅

This is the single most important factor. Under the Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, 1970 and the new Labour Codes 2026, every manpower supplier must:

  • Have a valid Labour Licence under CLRA
  • Register all deployed workers under EPF (Employee Provident Fund)
  • Provide ESI (Employee State Insurance) coverage for medical benefits
  • Maintain proper attendance registers, wage slips, and challan records
⚠️ If a manpower supplier cannot show you their EPF/ESI registration numbers and monthly challan receipts, walk away immediately. Non-compliance makes YOU (the principal employer) legally liable.

2. Verify Worker Background & Skills 🔍

A professional labour contractor in Mirzapur should provide:

  • Aadhar-verified identity for every worker
  • Skill certificates — ITI diplomas, wireman licenses, NCVT certificates
  • Police verification for security-sensitive deployments
  • Previous work experience records

Never accept workers without proper identity verification, especially for construction projects or factory deployments where safety is paramount.

3. Safety Training & PPE Standards 🦺

Construction and industrial sites in Mirzapur — from Chunar sandstone quarries to Vindhyachal commercial corridors — involve significant safety risks. Your manpower supplier must:

  • Provide safety helmets, shoes, gloves, and harnesses
  • Conduct toolbox safety briefings before work starts
  • Have a documented Safety SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)
  • Maintain first-aid kits and emergency contacts on site

5. Transparent Pricing with No Hidden Costs 💰

Beware of manpower suppliers who quote low daily rates but add hidden charges later. A transparent supplier will provide:

  • Written Service Agreement before deployment starts
