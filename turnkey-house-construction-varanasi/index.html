How VDA Approvals, Soil Strata & Micro-Markets Differ Across Varanasi

Independent plot construction in Varanasi varies sharply by zone. In BHU, Lanka, Mahmoorganj, and Sigra, high population density and narrow feeder roads make plot-front material staging and VDA ground-coverage compliance critical. Along Shivpur, Tarna, Babatpur, and Ring Road Phase II, rapid plotted colony growth requires checking whether your land holds Section 80 (old Section 143) non-agricultural conversion before filing online VDA building sanction drawings. Near Ramnagar, Sarnath, and Dashashwamedh–Nakkhighat, our structural engineers check flood-level plinth heights and ASI / National Monuments Authority 100m/300m regulated-zone radius clearances prior to excavation.
