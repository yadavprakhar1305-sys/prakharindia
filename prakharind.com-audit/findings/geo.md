# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & AI Search Citability

## Entity Citability Assessment

With Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT search dominating property queries, content must be structured for direct citation extraction.

### Strengths Identified:
1. **Machine-Readable Markdown Endpoints:** Both `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` exist and provide factual data points with bulleted entity attributes, contact details, district hubs, and exact pricing cards.
2. **Deterministic Pricing Data:** Real estate queries strongly reward deterministic rates. Prakhar India explicitly states 2026 rates (Standard ₹1,650/sq ft, Premium ₹2,100/sq ft, Luxury ₹2,650+/sq ft).
3. **Crawler Ingestion Clearances:** Robots.txt allows:
   - `OAI-SearchBot`
   - `GPTBot`
   - `PerplexityBot`
   - `Claude-SearchBot`
   - `Google-Extended`
4. **Natural Language Q&A Anchors:** The site features dedicated FAQ accordions addressing typical conversational queries:
   - *"What is the house construction cost per sq ft in UP in 2026?"*
   - *"Are Prakhar India plots RERA approved?"*
   - *"How to calculate turnkey construction cost in Varanasi?"*

### Citability Score: 96 / 100
Prakhar India is in the top tier of local civil contracting websites prepared for AI search engine answers.
